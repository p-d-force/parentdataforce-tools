HOW TO USE

Three steps. No account needed.

01

Paste the URL

Copy the full YouTube URL from your browser address bar and paste it into the input field above. Both youtube.com/watch?v= and youtu.be/ links work.

02

Read & Search

The full transcript appears with clickable timestamps. Use the search box to find specific words or phrases — matching lines are highlighted and the count updates live.

03

Copy or Download

Click Copy All to copy the full transcript to your clipboard. Use the download buttons to save as a plain text file (.txt) or a subtitle file (.srt) for use in video editors or players.

OUR STANDARD

We do not store transcripts.

Transcripts are fetched on demand and returned directly to your browser. Nothing is logged, cached, or stored on our servers. This tool is for research and accessibility purposes — always respect video creators' rights and platform terms.
