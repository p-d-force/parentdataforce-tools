HOW WE SOURCE

From the documents up.

Stories are grounded in the primary record. We publish the source so you can check our work.

School Committee

Minutes, agendas, and votes of the Worcester School Committee — the public record of every decision.

  • Agendas and minutes are read cover to cover
  • Votes are checked against the meeting video when available
  • Written by the source documents of the reports

Public Records

Records requests, DESE filings, budgets, and contracts — the documents districts would rather stay quiet.

  • Every claim traced to a named document
  • Records requests tracked on the public-records board
  • Redaction and tampering checks on every PDF
More source categories — budgets, discipline data, and staffing — are being added.
