WHAT WE DO

A watchdog for the paperwork.

Most education disputes are won or lost in the documents. We make those documents legible — and hold districts accountable when they aren't.

✓

Evidence-first advocacy

We verify every claim against DESE letters, court filings, and official records. No hearsay, no speculation — just the record.

✓

Tools that protect privacy

OCR, conversion, and forensics run server-side and files are deleted after processing. The rest of our tools never leave your browser.

✓

Local accountability

We track what's happening in Worcester's schools — minutes, records, and the stories hidden in them — so families aren't reading alone.
